Chess piece artwork
===================

The piece images in this folder are the "Merida" set by Armando Hernandez
Marroquin, as published in the lichess project
(https://github.com/lichess-org/lila, public/piece/merida).

License: GNU General Public License, version 2 or (at your option) any later
version (GPLv2+). See https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.html

Changes: the Chess Trainer recolours the pieces when it loads them (cream for
White, near-black for Black), resizes them, and rotates the king for the
checkmate animation. The files in this folder are unchanged originals. If you
share this app, keep this notice and the original files with it.
